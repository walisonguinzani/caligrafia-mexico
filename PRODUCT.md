# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS, one page plus assets folder (fast on mobile from Meta ads, deployable anywhere, e.g. Vercel like the owner's other pages).

## Users

Mexican adults (mostly women) arriving from Meta ads on their phone who dislike their handwriting and want to write "bonito"; also parents and teachers looking for handwriting practice for children. Spanish (Mexico), informal "tú".

## Product Purpose

Sell the "Paquete Completo de Caligrafía" (Material de Caligrafía desde Cero): printable PDF workbooks to learn and practice calligraphy from the fundamentals, plus a children's section and an app with video lessons. Success = visitor taps the checkout CTA.

## Positioning

One single price of $200 MXN for everything (adults + kids + app). The funnel's promise: "primero recibes todo el material, lo revisas y solo después pagas". Presented by María, warm and personal voice.

## Operating Context

Visitors come from a 9:16 video ad (before/after handwriting). Printing at home, practicing with a Bic pen or pencil, at their own pace. Content delivered as PDFs plus a members app with video lessons (screen recording on hand).

## Capabilities and Constraints

Deliverables (real, on hand):
- Adultos – Capítulo 1 Bienvenida y fundamentos (36 pp), Capítulo 2 ABC Lineal (72 pp), Capítulo 3 Copperplate (150 pp), Capítulo 4 Blackletter/Gótica (120 pp), Capítulo 5 Script/Brush Lettering (130 pp)
- Caligrafía Fácil para Todos (418 pp)
- Frases Motivadoras: 55 plantillas de brush lettering (56 pp)
- Niños: Preescritura (41 pp), Cursiva e Imprenta (103 pp), Lectoescritura (308 pp), Cursiva Integral (81 pp)
- Aplicación con videoclases (one combined benefit)
- Not part of this offer (future upsells, never mention on the page): grupo VIP de WhatsApp, acceso vitalicio/de por vida
Checkout: will be integrated via "Zenith API" (undecided details); CTA URL is a configurable placeholder.
Landing page offer: pago único $200 MXN via checkout, garantía de 30 días (reembolso). "Recibe primero, paga después" is WhatsApp-only: never on the landing page.

## Brand Commitments

Voice from the WhatsApp funnel: María, cercana, "¡Que Dios te bendiga!", emojis ✍️✨ sparingly. Price $200 MXN. Mexican flag used in the ad.

## Evidence on Hand

PDF pages (real), members-area screen recording, ad before/after image. No testimonials yet: do not fabricate any. No earnings/result guarantees.

## Product Principles

- Show the real material; pages prove value better than claims.
- One price, one action, no confusion.
- Warm, personal, trustworthy: no hype tricks, no fake scarcity counters.
