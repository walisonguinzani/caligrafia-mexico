# Design: Caligrafía desde Cero

World: lettering and practice sheets. Ink-blue cover bands (taken from the workbook covers) alternate with ruled practice paper. Headlines mix a condensed grotesque with one handwritten word.

## Color
- Tinta `#0F1A4A` (cover bands: hero, app, price, footer) · Tinta pluma `#1C2C7A` (script words, ink)
- Oro `#D4AE68` (CTA on dark, script on dark, ornament frame) · Oro claro `#F1DDB0`
- Papel `#FFFFFF` · Papel azul `#F3F5FB` · Guía `#C9D3EE` (ruled lines) · Trazo `#D2453B` (red margin and start strokes, used sparingly)

## Type
- Yellowtail: the handwritten word in each headline, group labels, signature.
- Bricolage Grotesque (condensed ~88%, 700–800): headlines, prices, buttons.
- Figtree: body.
- Pinyon Script and UnifrakturMaguntia appear only as style samples (Copperplate, Gótica) and the price card name.

## Signature
"Escribe tu nombre": the visitor's name rendered on practice lines in Lineal, Copperplate, Gótica and Brush, each tied to its chapter. The hero word "hermosa" writes itself in once.

## Components
- Ruled sheet: baseline, dashed x-height and ascender lines, red margin.
- Workbook shelf: real covers as books; selected one gets a gold ring; dark viewer shows real pages in a scroll-snap strip.
- Buttons: gold pill on dark, ink pill on light.
